System Navigation Gestures
Go to Settings > System \& updates > System navigation and make sure that Gestures is selected.
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|}
  & Return to the previous screen Swipe in from the left or right edges to return to the previous screen. \\
  & Back to home screen Swipe up from the bottom to go to the home screen. \\
  & Recent tasks Swipe up from the bottom of the screen and hold to view recent tasks. \\
  & Close an app When viewing recent tasks, swipe up on an app preview to close the app. \\
 
\end{tabular}
